# Sources

Where the files came from, when each episode came out, and what was said about the series at the time. Forum links
go to the [Anarchaeology forum archive](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/), Funcom's 2001–2003 forums as the
Wayback Machine saved them; its times are GMT, as the forum showed them.

## The episodes

| Episode | Title | Parts | Length | Released |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 101 | Tricks and Treats | 5 (a–e) | 16:38 | Wed 31 Oct 2001 |
| 102 | Burning Bridges | 5 (a–e) | 15:42 | Wed 14 Nov 2001 (on the forum the next day) |
| 103 | Reversal | 3 (a–c) | 7:51 | Wed 28 Nov 2001 |
| 104 | Dust to Dust | 4 (a–d) | 11:34 | Tue 18 Dec 2001 (Funcom's date; on the forum the next day) |

A new episode every two weeks, on Wednesdays, until 104 was moved to go with a game patch. There was never an
Episode 105.

## When each episode came out

The forum archive has the 2001 launch forum up to 27 Sep 2001, and Funcom's main forum from the day it opened,
15 Nov 2001. Nothing survives from between, so the forum says nothing about 101's release.

- **101, Wed 31 Oct 2001**, Halloween, which the episode is about.
  - GameSpot's "Anarchy Online story begins" (31 Oct 2001, 3:55 PM PST) says the storyline "begins today", with the
    first episode on the official story site.
  - GameSpot's storyline impressions that week open "On Wednesday, October 31, Funcom began the storyline arc".
  - Funcom's FTP folder `/storyline/` (as the Wayback Machine saved it on 2 Nov 2001) held only the preview of the
    opening titles, uploaded 30 Oct. Players watched 101 as Windows Media files from the story site; the MPEG parts
    went onto the FTP later.
  - A player recalls in July 2002: "we were told October 31 and everyone logged on then"
    ([thread 39274](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/39274.html)).
- **102, Wed 14 Nov 2001.** On 15 Nov, within two hours of the new forum opening, players have the new episode and
  ask where its playlist is: [Were is the playlist for the second set of story videos?](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/89.html)
  (15 Nov 2001 17:12).
- **103, Wed 28 Nov 2001.** [Chess Game](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/2247.html) (28 Nov 2001
  17:37) picks apart the moves called in 103's chess game.
- **104, Tue 18 Dec 2001.**
  - Funcom moved it from Wed 12 Dec "because it contains links with the 13.4 patch":
    [Episode 4 - postponed...](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/3897.html) (12 Dec 2001), and
    [Is today patch day Cosmik/CZ?](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/3877.html) (12 Dec 2001).
  - The forum's first "Go watch Episode 4" is 19 Dec 2001 12:00, in
    [Agh! Keep Episodes Alive!](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/3903-6.html#post254940); then
    [lol .. wrong link in mpeg 104 episodes](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/4582.html) (12:19) and
    [Episode 4](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/4632.html) (15:38).
  - Patch 13.4 went live the same day and put the episode's bombed apartment block into Omni-1 Entertainment.

## After 104

- [Ok, it's official. No more animated series :(](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/5535.html)
  (28 Dec 2001) points to the news on Ragnar Tørnquist's own site.
- [Save the animated series petition](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/15871.html) (15 Mar 2002).
- [Ragnar's Story where has it gone ?](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/8205.html) (9 Apr 2002). Tørnquist
  [says](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/8205.html#post384370) he had little to do with the series' success, and credits the artists, animators
  and audio staff, who made "almost one hour of top-quality CGI in two months".
- [I miss the animated series!](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/39274.html) (19 Jul 2002). Ragnar
  Tørnquist, the series' writer and co-director, [answers](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/39274.html#post573038)
  why it stopped. The episodes had plenty of viewers. But 10–15 minutes every two weeks was beyond the team's means:
  "The animation-team almost killed themselves getting the first four episodes out on time", working "16-18 hour
  days, plus weekends". He also says it was made by the team of Funcom's *Midgard* after that project was put on hold,
  so it took nothing from the Anarchy Online team, and that it was pre-rendered because the game's engine couldn't do
  cinematics or lip-sync.
- Players kept asking: [Any ETA on Episode 105?](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/41009.html)
  (28 Jul 2002, no answer from Funcom); [Animated Series](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/62837.html)
  (27 Oct 2002), would it come back after the *Notum Wars* expansion?

## Snarf on Radiman's desk

Snarf was a real player: an Atrox Nano-Technician, leader of The Shadows of Rubi-Ka, who
[declared himself Mayor of Tir](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/2058.html) (27 Nov 2001). In 104 a sheet
with his picture and name lies on Henry Radiman's desk (104 at 4:08; 41:25 in the video of the whole series). It
fits GameSpot's note on 31 Oct that Funcom hoped to bring "central players from the game" into the episodes.

- [Snarf? On Radiman's Desk? Oh Cute!](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/4592.html) (19 Dec 2001
  13:26, the day it came out); Snarf himself replies at 16:13.
- [Snarf is famous.](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/4662.html) (19 Dec 2001)
- Found again by later players: [Hahaha... SNARF, did you notice?](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/27152.html)
  (27 May 2002), [Snarf?!](https://anarchaeology.github.io/forums/thread/55534.html) (30 Sep 2002).

## The source files

- **What:** Funcom's MPEG release of all 17 parts, `aoe101a.mpg` … `aoe104d.mpg`, as Funcom's FTP held them. The
  largest and best of Funcom's formats; the others were Windows Media (high and low) and DivX.
- **From:** [Timba's Anarchy Online collection](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0BxuVjelTHvdWRFQ1eE1WQmNkYXc?resourcekey=0-_ioV-8mB3r1TwCgzzbNinw) on Google Drive, a public archive of concept art, game discs
  and Funcom's files, introduced in [a thread on Funcom's forum](https://forums.funcom.com/t/anarchy-online-concept-art-collection-looking-for-more-missing-items/6653). It saved the contents of Funcom's FTP server in
  July 2016, the episodes among them; Timba's [changelog](https://forums.funcom.com/t/anarchy-online-concept-art-collection-looking-for-more-missing-items/6653/2) thanks Yumehime for them.
- **Checksums:** [log/originals.tsv](log/originals.tsv) (size and MD5 of each file).
- **Whole:**
  - The sizes on Funcom's download pages for 101, 103 and 104 match these files, all 12 parts. 102's page repeats
    101's sizes, a slip in the page.
  - 102's five files end with MPEG's end-of-stream code, which a cut-off download wouldn't have.
  - All 17 decode without an error, and each is the same length as the Internet Archive's copy, within 0.15 s.
- **Complete:** Funcom's download pages list exactly these 17 parts and no others.
